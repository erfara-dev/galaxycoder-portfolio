01Overview

A configurable modular template

Universal Website is an ongoing PHP Native project to develop a flexible website template adaptable to different business requirements. Its architecture is planned around independent BOOK and BUY modules, with customization designed around structured configuration files rather than a dedicated dashboard.

The reusable Core is being extracted from LapanganKu. BOOK is the first module being developed from that foundation. Universal Website remains a work in progress, not a completed SaaS platform or production-ready product.
