01Overview

An early booking concept

VoliBooking was developed during Class XI as an early sports-field booking website. It introduced a digital booking concept built around a single-vendor business model and was an important milestone in the developer's journey.

The original source code is no longer available. This case study is limited to the confirmed project context, architecture and technical focus rather than unverified implementation details.

03Historical significance

From single-vendor concept to multivendor platform

VoliBooking was the original single-vendor booking concept. LapanganKu later evolved from its initial ideas into a more comprehensive multivendor platform.

They are distinct projects with significantly different features, business workflows and system architecture. VoliBooking is an important foundation in the project journey—not a failed project or an earlier implementation of the same system.
