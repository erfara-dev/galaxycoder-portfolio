01Overview

A customer ordering experience

This experimental project began during a school UI/UX design lesson. The initial interface was designed in Google Stitch, then developed with React and Vite using Google AI Studio for AI-assisted development.

Its focus is a mobile-friendly customer-facing ordering interface for a local food business concept—not a production food service or a native Android or iOS application.

02Customer-facing scope

Food discovery and ordering

The documented interface scope covers a home dashboard with a promotional banner, food categories and menu; an add-to-cart interaction and shopping cart with quantity controls; and user profile and bottom navigation.

These are the confirmed customer-facing scope areas. The owner-provided visual depicts the home, cart and profile screens, but the original application source is not included in this portfolio repository, so the behavior of individual controls could not be independently inspected.

04Limitations

An experiment, not a production service

The scope is limited to the customer-facing interface. No vendor dashboard or administrator dashboard is included.

This project is not presented as production-deployed. No completed backend integration, real payment processing, restaurant operations, or actual customer transactions are claimed. It is a mobile-focused React and Vite prototype, not a native Android or iOS application.
